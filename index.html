Privacy Policy — Pocket EMT®

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Medical and emergency-use disclaimer

Pocket EMT® is an educational and reference resource. It does not diagnose medical conditions, is not a medical device or clinical decision-support tool, and does not replace emergency services, qualified healthcare professionals, or appropriate training. It should never delay contacting emergency services. In a life-threatening emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.

About this policy

This policy explains how Excelsior Protect® handles information when you use Pocket EMT® (the “App”). The consumer app is designed to provide emergency guidance offline where available, without consumer account registration, advertising, or consumer subscriptions. Some features, such as opening an external map or making a purchase, involve third-party services as described below.

1. Data controller and contact

Excelsior Protect® is responsible for handling personal data described in this policy.

Privacy enquiries

2. Information the App handles

  • Account information: The consumer version does not require account creation or sign-in.
  • Emergency guidance and triage answers: Information entered during a guidance workflow is used to provide the on-screen guidance. It is intended to remain temporary in the active app session and is not intended to create a patient record or history.
  • Location: If you choose a location feature and grant the required device permission, the App may access your device's current location to support location display or sharing. The App does not provide continuous location tracking or a location-history feature.
  • Language and app preferences: Certain preferences and offline resources may be stored locally on your device.
  • Purchase information: If you buy the one-time unlock, the transaction is handled by Apple App Store or Google Play billing. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details through the App.
  • Technical information: The operating system, app store, or platform may process technical and diagnostic information under its own terms. The exact information depends on the platform and configuration.

The App is not designed to collect or store medical records, patient profiles, or triage history.

3. How information is used

Information is used to provide the App's emergency-reference functions, respond to an action you initiate, remember applicable local preferences, enable and restore store purchases, maintain app security, and meet legal obligations where applicable.

We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising in the consumer App.

4. Location, maps, and emergency calls

  • Location access is optional and depends on your device permissions and the feature you choose.
  • If you choose Open in Map or another external map action, relevant location information may be sent to the map provider you open, under that provider's privacy terms.
  • Assisted Emergency Calling, where available and unlocked, is initiated only after an explicit user action. The phone call action passes the selected telephone number to the device's calling function; the App does not automatically place emergency calls.
  • The App does not automatically transmit triage answers or patient details as part of the telephone number used to initiate a call.

5. Triage and sensitive information

Triage responses are used to display guidance during the active workflow. The App is designed not to save triage answers as a persistent patient record or history. Do not enter information that is not needed to use the emergency guidance. If you choose to share information with emergency responders or another service outside the App, that sharing is governed by the action you take and the recipient's practices.

6. Legal basis and applicable rights

Where data protection law applies, the legal basis depends on the specific processing and may include providing a feature you request, legitimate interests such as app security, consent for optional device permissions, or compliance with a legal obligation.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive certain personal data, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact us using the details in Section 13 to make a request.

7. Storage, security, and retention

App preferences and supported offline resources may be stored locally on your device. Store transaction records are also handled by the relevant app-store provider. We apply reasonable measures appropriate to the systems and processing under our control; no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

The consumer App is not intended to maintain patient records, triage histories, or location histories. Technical logs, if generated by the operating system, app store, hosting provider, or other service, may be retained according to that provider's policies.

8. Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The consumer App is designed without advertising and without consumer-facing tracking or analytics. Local app storage may be used for preferences and offline functionality. The public website or third-party services used to access this policy may process standard technical data necessary to deliver and secure their services, according to their own configuration and policies.

9. Third-party services and sharing

Information may be processed by a third party when you choose to use its service, including Apple App Store or Google Play for billing, an external map provider when you open a map, or your device's operating system for telephone functionality. Those providers act under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information or share it with third parties for their own targeted advertising through the consumer App.

10. International transfers

Third-party platform providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their handling of that information is governed by their applicable terms and safeguards. We do not make a blanket claim that all processing by every third-party provider occurs exclusively within the European Economic Area.

11. Children

Pocket EMT® is an emergency education and reference app, not a service designed to collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian should supervise a child's use where appropriate. If you believe personal information has been provided to us in a way that requires action, contact us.
